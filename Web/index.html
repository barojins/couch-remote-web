Movement

About these settings

Speed changes travel distance. Acceleration adds speed to fast swipes; set it to zero for a consistent ratio. Smoothing steadies acceleration changes without adding glide after lifting your finger. Scroll speed adjusts two-finger scrolling and short scroll-button taps. Holding a scroll button always uses maximum speed. Settings are saved on this phone.

Help

Connect

Keep the Mac awake and all devices on the same private Wi-Fi. Scan the Mac’s QR code or enter its 8-digit connection code. Compare the approval codes, then choose Trust this phone on the Mac. Multiple phones can stay connected; approve each new phone separately.

Trackpad and keyboard

Slide to move, tap to click, double-tap to double-click. Tap with two fingers to right-click, or slide two fingers to scroll. Use the two buttons on the right to scroll: tap for a small step, or hold for continuous scrolling at maximum speed. Release to stop. Scroll speed in Movement settings adjusts short taps and two-finger scrolling. Below the trackpad, use the left/right keys to navigate and the middle button to play or pause the Mac’s active media app. For search, first select the search field on your Mac with the trackpad. Type in the search box on this page, then tap Search or your phone’s Search key. This sends the text followed by Enter; it does not read or replace existing text on the Mac. Your phone’s own keyboard opens when you tap the box.

Use without Safari’s toolbar

In Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Open that icon and approve it once on the Mac.

Volume

These buttons control the Mac’s volume keys. HDMI audio may require the TV’s own volume controls.

Privacy

This remote uses local HTTP. Use trusted private Wi-Fi only; avoid sending passwords or sensitive text and never forward the receiver port to the internet.
