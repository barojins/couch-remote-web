Movement

About these settings

Speed changes travel distance. Acceleration adds speed to fast swipes; set it to zero for a consistent ratio. Smoothing steadies acceleration changes without adding glide after lifting your finger. Settings are saved on this phone.

Help

Connect

Keep the Mac awake and both devices on the same private Wi-Fi. Scan the Mac’s QR code or enter its 8-digit connection code. Compare the approval codes, then choose Trust this phone on the Mac.

Trackpad and keyboard

Slide to move, tap to click, double-tap to double-click. Tap with two fingers to right-click, or slide two fingers to scroll. For typing, select a text field on the Mac, then open Keyboard.

Use without Safari’s toolbar

In Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Open that icon and approve it once on the Mac.

Volume

These buttons control the Mac’s volume keys. HDMI audio may require the TV’s own volume controls.

Privacy

This remote uses local HTTP. Use trusted private Wi-Fi only; avoid sending passwords or sensitive text and never forward the receiver port to the internet.
